# Kill the Cockroaches!

# Powered by Hive-r
# Created by Iver S.

**Kill the Cockroaches!** is a fun and interactive browser-based game where players smash cockroaches to score points. The game features dynamic animations, sound effects, and a responsive design for an engaging experience.

---

## Features
- **Dynamic Gameplay**: Smash cockroaches as they appear on the screen.
- **Timer and Scoring**: Keep track of your kills and time remaining.
- **Interactive UI**: Vibrant animations and hover effects.
- **Offline Support**: Play even without an internet connection.
- **Installable**: Add the game to your device as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

---

## How to Play
1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Click the **Start Game** button to begin.
3. Smash as many cockroaches as you can before the timer runs out.
4. Check your score and play again to beat your high score!
